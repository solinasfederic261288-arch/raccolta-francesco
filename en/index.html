Why this fundraiser

My name is Federico and I live in Sardinia. I was the first case in Sardinia of clear cell chondrosarcoma, a rare and malignant bone tumour. I went to Florence for treatment and paid for everything myself.

Today there's a second case on our island: Francesco, who is 19. He too has to go to Florence for treatment, but he and his family can't afford the trip.

I've already been down that road, and I know how heavy it is to have to organise it. That's why I decided to ask for help from anyone who can give it.

This fundraiser is for one thing only: paying for Francesco's and his family's trip to Florence. The goal is €754.

Every contribution counts, however small. And if you can't donate right now, sharing this page is already a huge help.

Thank you from the bottom of my heart.

— Federico

Francesco agreed to appear with his name and photo. Out of respect for their privacy, his family remains anonymous.
